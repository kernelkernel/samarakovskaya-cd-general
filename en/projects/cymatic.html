The geometry of sound waves — visualized on the facade of classical architecture.

Cymatic is projection mapping on the facade of the Bolshoi Theatre. The concept is rooted in cymatics: the visualization of sound waves as geometric patterns.

Classical architecture becomes a surface for generative visual content. Crystalline structures and wave patterns grow through the columns and reliefs, turning the facade into a living screen.

The projection works as a dialogue between architecture and sound: the geometry of the building reinforces the rhythmic logic of the visuals, while the cymatics patterns respond to the spatial scale of the theatre.

02 / Execution

Sound as visuals.

Generative · Mapping
Bolshoi Theatre
01
Object · 01

The facade as a surface

The columns, reliefs and portico of the Bolshoi Theatre become elements of the visual composition. The mapping works with the architectural relief: the projection is not laid over the building but built into its geometry, reinforcing every volume and shadow.

02
Content · 02

Generative patterns

The show’s content is built on the principles of cymatics — the visualization of acoustic frequencies as geometric structures. Crystalline patterns shift in real time, following the musical score. Each note has its own geometry.

03
Audience · 03

The square as an auditorium

The theatre square in front of the building becomes an open-air auditorium. The projection works over a long distance and reads as a large-scale street show — without the usual frame of a screen or stage.

Content for the facade

The visuals were made with the architectural relief of the Bolshoi Theatre in mind: columns, portico and volumes are part of the composition. The content was fitted to the mapping and the scale of the square.
