“CapybaraBooom” is a character-led universe built from scratch: characters, a world, a phygital exhibition and a wider ecosystem, all developed from one question — why did the world fall in love with capybaras?

Capybaras took over social media for a reason: in a world of anxiety and overload, their image became the perfect meme for the moment. The Moskvarium exhibition turns this cultural phenomenon into a high-emotion space — alive, playful, visually bright and kawaii.

The format is edutainment: scientific facts are embedded in the mechanics. Visitors don’t just read panels — they act, compete and leave with a story.

04✦

Exhibition zones.

01
Intro · interactive

Capybarization zone

The first zone of the exhibition: visitors go through a virtual “capybarization” process — creating their own character, collecting capycoins and finding out who becomes the ultimate capy. The route starts here — and immediately pulls them in.

Onboarding · interactive
02
Edutainment zone

Capybarius zone

An interactive space where scientific facts about capybaras are delivered through pop-culture language. The zone is guided by Professor Capybarius, with CapyQuiz and game mechanics instead of static panels.

Capybarius · edutainment
03
360° panorama

The Amazon zone

A circular 360° projection screen in a roller-coaster format: an interactive journey down the Amazon with sharp turns and a dynamic boat visitors can steer. The most thrilling point of the route.

360° · roller coaster
04
Sports zone

The CapyBro zone

Visitors discover capybaras’ athletic abilities by competing against them. Turn the cranks, jump higher, test your aim and challenge friends or family. At the center is CapyBro, lifting a bamboo barbell instead of weight plates.

Sports zone
05
Zen zone

Capybara-san

A meditating capybara above a glowing lotus. An interactive projection with several modes — a quiet pause in the middle of the route.

Glowing lotus · orb
06
Fun zone

The Capybara-boy zone

Capybaraoke, arcade machines and a dance floor with LED flooring and motion tracking. Sing, dance and play — the most playful spot in the exhibition.

Arcades · karaoke · dancing
07
Finale · photo zone

Tangerine booom

A large pool of orange tangerine-like balls — capybaras’ favorite treat and a viral meme turned into a huge photo zone and physical space. After the digital zones, the route shifts into movement, brightness and activity.

Ball pit

The 4 final characters grew out of dozens of alternative versions. Each capybara has its own world, backstory and silhouette logic — from a hand-drawn sketch to a 3D render ready for the exhibition space and the merch line.

Floats above a lotus. Brings visitors into a state of zen — effortlessly and without words.
